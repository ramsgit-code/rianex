GoHighLevel and HubSpot are compared a lot and have little in common. GoHighLevel is an all-in-one designed to capture leads, book appointments and talk to customers by WhatsApp, SMS and voice, at a fixed price in dollars. HubSpot is a modular CRM that grows by seats and by "hubs", with better reporting and a much larger ecosystem in Spanish.

We implement both and have migrated clients from one to the other. This comparison uses the prices published on their official websites as of 18 November 2026; both change often, so confirm them before signing.

## The short answer

- **GoHighLevel**, if you live off capturing leads, giving appointments and answering on WhatsApp, with short sales cycles: clinics, estate agencies, appointment-based businesses and marketing agencies.
- **HubSpot**, if you sell to companies with long cycles, need serious sales reports or want a CRM in Spanish that the team can handle without intensive training.

If you already use one and it works for you, it almost always works out better to [integrate what is missing](/en/blog/integrar-gohighlevel-con-tu-stack) than to change CRM.

## What each one really costs

**GoHighLevel.** The plan price is the easy part. Starter costs $97/month and Unlimited $297/month. On top of that comes what you use: WhatsApp costs $10/month per sub-account plus Meta's fees per conversation. Conversation AI is charged by model tokens. Voice AI charges $0.045/min for the engine and $0.015/min for the voice, not counting the model or the telephony, so a 10-minute call comes to around $0.60 before those two. With low volume it is cheap; with high volume, it is worth doing the sums.

**HubSpot.** The free plan is enough to start with two users. Starter is at €7/month per seat on a temporary 65% offer, and Pro at €90/month per seat, paid annually. Moving to Pro or Enterprise means paying for onboarding, the amount of which the pricing page does not publish. Breeze AI credits are included, but they do not roll over from one month to the next.

The typical calculation: with a small team and a high volume of messages, GoHighLevel usually works out cheaper. With many users on Pro plans, HubSpot climbs quickly.

## GoHighLevel: what it does well and its limits

**What it does well.** It joins in one tool what elsewhere would be five: CRM, funnels and forms, booking calendars, WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice with AI, all connected to the same automations. For a business that lives off answering fast and filling the calendar, that is almost everything it needs. We explain it in [what GoHighLevel is and when it pays off](/en/blog/que-es-gohighlevel).

**Honest limitations.**
- AI is charged separately from the plan, by usage: tokens, minutes and telephony. You have to watch consumption.
- The Spanish translation of the interface is incomplete, and training in Spanish is scarce.
- It is designed for agencies (sub-accounts): an SME on its own pays for features it does not use.
- Reporting is basic and falls short in B2B sales with several stages and decision-makers.

## HubSpot: what it does well and its limits

**What it does well.** It is the most polished CRM for managing a sales process: stages, tasks, forecasts and reports. It has its website, pricing, support and documentation in Spanish, and a huge ecosystem of integrations and of professionals who know it. Breeze, its AI, is included with monthly credits.

**Honest limitations.**
- Moving to Pro or Enterprise forces you to pay for onboarding on top of the seats.
- The Starter price you see today is a temporary offer.
- Native WhatsApp requires a WhatsApp Business account and, in the experience of those who implement it, Pro plans.
- The calendar and messaging are designed for sales meetings rather than for giving hundreds of appointments a week.

## Choose GoHighLevel if…

…your business lives off leads from ads and portals, off giving appointments and off WhatsApp; you sell in short cycles; and you prefer a fixed price with everything included to paying per user.

## Choose HubSpot if…

…you sell to companies with cycles of weeks or months; you need sales reports for management; your sales team is growing; or you want a CRM anyone can use in Spanish from day one.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is GoHighLevel cheaper than HubSpot?

With few users and a high volume of lead capture and messages, usually yes: the plan is fixed and you do not pay per user. But AI, WhatsApp and telephony are charged separately by usage. With many users on HubSpot Pro, the difference in GoHighLevel's favour grows; with two users on HubSpot free or Starter, HubSpot can work out cheaper.

### Does HubSpot have WhatsApp?

Yes, as a channel in the conversations inbox, by connecting a WhatsApp Business account. It is intended for higher plans. In GoHighLevel, WhatsApp is integrated into the automations for a monthly fee per sub-account.

### Is GoHighLevel in Spanish?

The interface can be set to Spanish, but the translation is incomplete and there is little training and support in Spanish. HubSpot is considerably better at this.

### Which has better AI?

They are different. GoHighLevel has agents that answer by chat and by voice and book appointments, charged by usage. HubSpot has Breeze, with an assistant and agents for content, customers and prospecting, included with monthly credits. For handling enquiries and booking, GoHighLevel goes further; for sales work inside the CRM, HubSpot.

### Can both be used at the same time?

They can, for example lead capture and booking in GoHighLevel and sales in HubSpot, synchronised with n8n or Make. It works, but it duplicates costs and points of failure: it only pays off if each one covers something the other does not do well.
