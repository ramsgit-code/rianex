More and more Spanish SMEs reach GoHighLevel by the same route: someone recommends it, they watch a video in English, they open a trial account and they find a panel full of menus they do not know how to approach.

The tool is not the problem. The problem is that GoHighLevel is sold as "all in one", and that is true, but it also means there is a lot to configure before it is useful for anything.

## The short answer

GoHighLevel is a platform that brings together in a single tool the CRM, sales pipelines, forms and funnels, messaging by email, SMS and WhatsApp, booking calendars, automations and review management. It was born for marketing agencies, and it shows. It pays off for SMEs with many leads, appointments and follow-up by WhatsApp: clinics, academies, events and local services. It does not pay off for companies with long, complex B2B sales, very advanced reporting or a team for whom HubSpot already works.

## What GoHighLevel is, piece by piece

It is several tools that are normally paid for separately and here share the same contact base.

- **CRM and pipelines.** Contacts, companies, opportunities and stages. Each opportunity moves across a board and each move can trigger an action.
- **Forms, funnels and websites.** Landing pages, surveys and forms that create the contact directly in the CRM, with no connectors in between.
- **Messaging.** Email, SMS and WhatsApp in a single inbox, with each contact's history on their record.
- **Calendars and bookings.** Booking links connected to the team's calendar, with automatic confirmations and reminders.
- **Automations (workflows).** The heart of the tool: "if this happens, do that", with waits, conditions and branches.
- **Reputation and reviews.** Automatic review requests after a service and management of the replies.
- **Conversational AI.** [An assistant that answers incoming messages, qualifies and books](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001335-conversation-ai-bot-explained "follow"). Useful when what sits underneath is already well set up.

The value is not in any single piece. The value is that they all see the same contact and can be chained together without external glue.

## Its origin: a platform for agencies

GoHighLevel was designed for marketing agencies to manage their clients. That is where two concepts come from that confuse anyone arriving as an end business.

**Sub-accounts.** Each business lives in a sub-account inside an agency account. If you sign up directly, you have an agency with a single sub-account, which is yours.

**White label.** Agencies can [resell GoHighLevel under their own name and domain](https://www.gohighlevel.com/white-label-crm "follow"). That is why an SME sometimes uses "its agency's CRM" without knowing that underneath it is GoHighLevel.

For an end business this means three things: there are agency menus you will never use, most of the content on the internet is written for agencies, and it is worth knowing who the account holder is. If your sub-account sits inside a supplier's agency, your data depends on that relationship.

Our recommendation is simple: the account should be in the name of the business, always.

## Who it fits

GoHighLevel works especially well when the business meets several of these conditions:

- Many leads come in through forms, ads or social media, and part of them are lost for lack of follow-up.
- It sells by appointment: consultations, assessments, visits, trial classes.
- A large part of the conversation with the customer happens on WhatsApp.
- The sales cycle is short or medium, days or a few weeks.

It fits clinics, academies, event companies, renovation firms, small estate agencies and local services in general. At a clinic's reception, for example, it joins lead capture, the appointment book and reminders in a single flow; we cover it in [how to automate a clinic's reception](/en/blog/automatizar-recepcion-clinica).

A real case: [Eventos Barcelona](/en/casos-de-exito/eventos-barcelona) runs its whole proposal pipeline in GoHighLevel. Before, they took between one and three days to send a proposal; now it reaches the client in about 8 minutes, and response time has dropped by 85%.

## Who it does not fit

There are cases where we do not recommend it, even though implementing it is what we do.

**Long, complex B2B sales.** If each deal involves several decision-makers, months of negotiation, quotes with many lines and internal approvals, other CRMs handle that better.

**Very advanced reporting.** GoHighLevel's reports cover the basics. If your management lives on dashboards with multi-channel attribution and forecasts per salesperson, it falls short and you will end up exporting data to another tool.

**A team that already lives in HubSpot and is doing well.** Changing CRM has a real cost in time and in habits. If the problem is that pieces are missing, it usually works out better to [integrate what is missing](/en/blog/integrar-gohighlevel-con-tu-stack) or complete the current CRM than to migrate.

## The weak points, plainly

**Learning curve.** It does many things and each has its options. The first weeks are hard, especially for whoever configures it.

**Dense interface.** There are many menus, many settings, and some are in unintuitive places.

**Documentation mostly in English.** The interface can be used in Spanish, but the tutorials, the knowledge base and the community are almost all in English. For many teams in Spain this is a real barrier.

**Support.** It works, but it is designed for agencies that know what they are asking. If you do not have someone who knows the tool, resolution times get longer.

Together they explain why so many accounts are left half configured.

## WhatsApp, properly connected

One of the reasons many SMEs choose GoHighLevel is WhatsApp. It is worth doing it the official way: the integration uses the [WhatsApp Business API](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001980-how-to-set-up-whatsapp-for-a-sub-account "follow"), not the phone app connected with workarounds.

That means verifying the number, working with [approved templates for the messages the business initiates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview "follow") and respecting the conversation windows. It costs more at the start and avoids the number being blocked.

## GDPR: where the data is

GoHighLevel is a US company, so the question of where your customers' data is hosted is legitimate and has to be asked before signing up.

The minimum: sign the [data processing agreement (DPA)](https://www.gohighlevel.com/data-processing-agreement "follow") the platform offers, review which mechanism covers international transfers, update your privacy policy and record each contact's consents in the CRM itself. We develop it in [GDPR and automation with AI](/en/blog/rgpd-y-automatizacion-con-ia).

## What an implementation looks like

A serious implementation almost always follows the same order:

1. **Assessment.** How you sell today, where contacts come in, how many are lost and where. If you do not have these numbers, start by [measuring the process before automating it](/en/blog/medir-un-proceso-antes-de-automatizarlo).
2. **Structure.** Fields, tags, pipelines and stages, before adding a single contact.
3. **Contact migration.** From spreadsheets or from another CRM, with deduplication.
4. **Channels and automations.** Forms, WhatsApp, email and calendar, and the automations nobody argues about: assign, acknowledge receipt, remind.
5. **Training.** The team working in the tool from day one, with only what they need to see.

On timelines: a small migration from another CRM is done in two or three weeks; implementing a first process from scratch takes between four and six; and with many channels and integrations it can run somewhat longer.

If it is your first automation project, the general order (centralise, automate the obvious and then add AI) is in [process automation with AI: where to start](/en/blog/automatizar-procesos-con-ia-por-donde-empezar).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GoHighLevel in a few words?

An all-in-one platform that joins CRM, pipelines, forms, funnels, messaging by email, SMS and WhatsApp, calendars, automations and reviews on the same contact base. It was born for marketing agencies and today end businesses use it too.

### Does GoHighLevel work in Spain?

Yes. The interface is available in Spanish and it can be used with Spanish numbers and customers. What is worth reviewing is the GDPR (DPA, international transfers and consents) and the fact that most of the documentation and training is in English.

### What are the reviews of GoHighLevel like?

The ratings tend to agree: very complete and with a good ratio between what it includes and what it costs, but with a steep learning curve and a dense interface. The most common criticisms are the support and that, without someone to configure it well, it is left half done.

### Is GoHighLevel or HubSpot better?

It depends on the business. GoHighLevel pays off with many leads, appointments and WhatsApp, and short sales cycles. HubSpot is better for long B2B sales, advanced reporting and teams that already use it with ease. The full comparison, with prices, is in [GoHighLevel vs HubSpot](/en/blog/gohighlevel-vs-hubspot).
