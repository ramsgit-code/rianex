WhatsApp is the most used sales channel in Spain and Latin America. It is also the most chaotic one in most companies. Messages with no reply, conversations with no history, leads lost because nobody saw them in time.

The difference between using it well and using it badly is not in the volume. It is in the system.

## The real problem

When WhatsApp works as a sales channel with no structure, three things happen:

Leads arrive and there is no immediate reply. On WhatsApp, a fast reply is not optional. The conversion studies are clear: a lead that gets no reply in the first 5 minutes is 80% less likely to convert than one that gets an immediate reply. Without automation, that depends on someone looking at the phone.

There is no central history. Each sales rep has conversations in their own WhatsApp. When someone leaves the company, the history disappears. When the customer gets in touch through another channel, the team does not know what was said before.

There is no structured follow-up. A lead that does not reply stays in the chat with no action. There is no automatic reminder, no follow-up sequence, no pipeline. Just a sent message that nobody chases.

## The practical solution

A WhatsApp system connected to GoHighLevel solves all three problems at once.

**Immediate reply 24/7**

The first contact is automatic. When a lead writes to the WhatsApp Business number, they get a reply in seconds: a personalised welcome, a first qualification question and, where it applies, a link to book or a form for their details.

The tone does not sound like a robot because it is not one. The flow uses variables from the contact's profile and replies differently depending on what the lead writes. It is not a rigid decision tree. It is a conversational flow.

**Qualification inside the chat**

The system asks the key questions inside the WhatsApp conversation. Type of need, available budget, urgency. Depending on the answers, the lead is classified in GHL with the matching tag and enters the right pipeline.

Qualified leads receive a Calendly link directly in the chat. Leads that need nurturing enter a follow-up sequence. The sales team only gets a notification when there is a qualified lead to book.

**Central history in the CRM**

The whole WhatsApp conversation is recorded in the contact's profile in GHL. Any member of the team can see what was said, when, and what was promised. If the lead gets in touch by email or phone, the team has the full context.

## How to implement it step by step

- Set up the WhatsApp Business API. Not the normal WhatsApp Business, but the API. You need a dedicated phone number and access through a provider (Meta directly or a provider such as Twilio). GHL has a native integration.
- Connect the number to GoHighLevel. Once the API is set up, GHL receives and sends messages from its platform. All conversations in one place.
- Design the welcome and qualification flow. The first 3-5 questions you need to know whether the lead is worth your time. In GHL this is set up as a workflow with conditions.
- Create the follow-up automations. If the lead does not reply within 24h, a reactivation message. If they qualify, a notification to the sales rep and a booking link. If they do not qualify, they enter email nurturing.
- Integrate with the sales pipeline. Each WhatsApp lead has its opportunity in the GHL pipeline with an initial stage, an assigned owner and automated follow-up.

## The result you can expect

Hermetic implemented this system and went from replying to WhatsApp leads in hours to having an automatic reply in seconds. The response rate of its leads went up because the first contact was immediate and personalised.

The team stopped wasting time on leads that were going nowhere. The system filtered them out first. And the good leads reached the team with context, already qualified, ready for a closing conversation.

0 leads without a reply in the first 24 hours. Not because the team worked more. Because the system worked when the team could not.
